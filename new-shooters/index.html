Everything you need for your first match

Your First Match

Practical shooting is a timed, moving, run-and-gun style of competition — and it’s the fastest way to get better with the firearms you already own. Here’s everything you need to show up to your first MAPSA match with confidence.

  1. Pick a match

    USPSA matches are pistol only. PCSL 2-Gun uses a pistol and a carbine. Check the 2026 schedule.

  2. Register

    Sign up on PractiScore (keyword MAPSA) and choose the division that matches your gear.

  3. Gear up

    Eye & ear protection, a holster that covers the trigger guard, magazines, pouches and ammo.

  4. Show up & shoot

    Arrive early, check in, and tell your squad it’s your first match. They’ll help you through it.

New Shooter FAQ

What is practical shooting?
Practical shooting is a timed sport where you move through a course of fire (a “stage”), engaging paper and steel targets from different positions. Your score balances accuracy and speed. Every stage is run one shooter at a time under a Range Officer, so safety is built in.
What is USPSA?
The United States Practical Shooting Association is the largest practical shooting organization in the country. USPSA matches are handgun matches with divisions for everything from stock pistols to fully customized race guns, so whatever you already own probably has a home. USPSA rules.
What is PCSL 2-Gun?
The Practical Competition Shooting League runs pistol and carbine matches. In 2-Gun you shoot both a pistol and a rifle/carbine, often on the same stage. PCSL website.
Do I need experience to come shoot?
No. You should be able to safely handle and draw your firearm from a holster, but you don’t need to be fast or have competed before. Tell your squad it’s your first match — practical shooters love helping new people get started.
What should I bring?
  • Eye and ear protection (required)
  • A pistol and a strong-side holster that fully covers the trigger guard
  • At least 3–4 magazines and magazine pouches
  • Enough ammo — the round count is listed on each match’s PractiScore page (plus extra)
  • For 2-Gun: a carbine/rifle, magazines, and a sling or case
  • Weather-appropriate clothes, water, and snacks — it’s Minnesota
How do I sign up for a match?
All MAPSA matches are listed on PractiScore (search keyword MAPSA). Create a free PractiScore account, pick the match, choose your division, and register. Spots can fill up, so register early.
Where are matches held?
4880 240th St N, Forest Lake, MN 55025 — use Gate 3. See the map on our schedule page.
What are the basic safety rules?
Matches run on a cold range: arrive with your firearm unloaded, and only handle it in designated safe areas or when a Range Officer directs you on the line. Keep your finger off the trigger and the muzzle pointed downrange. Breaking a safety rule means a disqualification for the day — nobody gets hurt, and you come back next time.
